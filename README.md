<!-- ======================= PROJECTS ======================= -->

## Selected Projects

### **FinPath - AI Financial Guidance Platform**
*Python, FastAPI, LangChain, Pinecone, Kafka, Kubernetes, GCP*  
I lost thousands sitting in a 0.5% savings account because visa-aware financial guidance didn't exist. So I built it.  
An AI assistant that answers visa-aware money questions grounded in cited regulations, and refuses to guess when the evidence is thin.

### **Healthcare RAG System**
*Python, FastAPI, LangChain, ChromaDB, Docker, Prometheus, Grafana*  
Most RAG projects stop at "it works." I built this one to answer the harder question: is the answer actually correct?  
A medical Q&A system over NIH MedlinePlus and PubMed that grounds every answer in real sources and gates every release on faithfulness.

### **TaskFlow - Distributed Job Scheduling Platform**
*Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker*  
Anyone can schedule a job. The hard part is finishing it after the worker crashes halfway through.  
A fault-tolerant scheduler that keeps jobs correct through failure, so nothing runs twice and nothing gets lost.

### **FlagGate - Feature Flag & Configuration Platform**
*Java, Spring Boot, Apache Cassandra, Apache Kafka, Docker*  
How do you flip a feature on in production without redeploying, and without slowing down a single request?  
That is the problem FlagGate solves, keeping every service in sync even after a broker failure.

### **FitMate.AI - Real-Time Pose Estimation**
*Python, MediaPipe, OpenCV, NVIDIA Jetson Nano*  
My first build on NVIDIA Jetson Nano, made to see how far real-time vision could go on hardware that small.  
FitMate watches your form and counts your reps live, gated behind face authentication so each session stays personalized.

### **SafeAbode.AI - Face-Recognition Door Camera**
*Python, OpenCV, NVIDIA Jetson Nano*  
Edge computer vision, pointed at my own front door. This build earned my NVIDIA Jetson AI Specialist certification.  
A door camera that recognizes repeat visitors and tracks how often and how long they linger, all on its own.

### **DebtCompass - Credit Risk Model**
*Python, XGBoost, LightGBM, SHAP, scikit-learn*  
Real credit data is messy and lopsided. I wanted to model it honestly, not on a clean textbook dataset.  
DebtCompass ranks borrowers by default risk and explains every call, so the model stays interpretable instead of a black box.
